## 1. Firecrawl industry-keyword campaigns hit blogs

**VERIFIED FACT (this snapshot):** 5 `prospecting_leads` in the Lovable DB came from a glamping / Östergötland Firecrawl campaign and resolved to travel blogs, not operating åkerier.

**INFERENCE:** A query built from industry + place keywords (`åkeri`, `glamping`, `Östergötland`, similar) ranks content sites and listicles above company homepages.

**Rule for next search:**
- Require an **operating company** (AB/HB with a commercial site that sells transport, not a magazine/blog).
- Require at least one **operational signal** on that site (see §4).
- Reject the row if the domain is a travel blog, destination guide, or "best of" list.

Do not treat Firecrawl row count as a qualified list.

## 2. CRM row counts are not pipeline

**VERIFIED FACT (2026-08-25 snapshot):** 3 contact (internal tests), 9 AI-karta (8 self/own brands, 1 demo), 0 genomlysning, 5 bad prospecting rows, close-existing-pipeline empty. Alex is not in this DB.

**Rule:**
- Qualified pipeline = named account + observed pain + allowed contact route + owner-accepted next action.
- Internal tests, own-brand AI-kartor, and demo names are activity, not demand.
- Empty CRM is not proof that no off-system relationship exists; it is only proof this DB has nothing to close.

## 3. Opter / Colix / Transiro = reject

**Rule:** If the public site (or an obvious customer-login host like `opter.cloud`) shows Opter, Colix, Transiro, or an equivalent live TMS/customer portal, **REJECT**. Do not spend research or drafts on "we can replace your TMS".

This batch's Kungsbud reject is the example (Opter). Alvinx / Dannes rejected as already digital. EA Åkeri/Tempcon and T. Malméns / Alltransport rejected as too large or already in a digital network.

Aurora Transport or a custom intake tool is a **later** conversation after a genomlysning, not a cold opener.

## 4. Best cheap signals (keep researching these)

Use public pages only. One strong signal beats three weak adjectives.

| Signal | Why it is cheap | Example this batch |
|---|---|---|
| Phone-only Boka | Order intake is a phone queue | G:sons "Ring Norrköping / Ring Linköping"; Lödöse "Ring direkt" |
| Email ≠ booking | Company admits mail/form is not confirmation | JS Åkeri contact page |
| 10–20 trucks | Enough admin to hurt, small enough to take a 20-min call | Lödöse 14; JS 10; KA ~20. C Anderssons 4 = weaker |
| Trafikledare + no portal | Named traffic role, customer still calls/mails | G:sons trafikledare; Östgöta Trafikchef; Sandberg `trafik@` |

Supporting but weaker: "skräddarsydd offert", generic `info@`, faktura mailbox (admin, not intake — do not sell to `faktura@`).
