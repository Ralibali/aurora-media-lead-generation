# GA4 measurement quality

Objective: owner-requested review and correction of GA4 measurement and goals across the active sites.
Authority: COMMIT / EMIT for the analytics corrections requested in this session. The owner explicitly chose to retain direct GA4 rather than migrate to GTM.

VERIFIED FACT: internal placement values appeared as traffic sources in the owner's GA4 reports for Updro and Hönsgården. All ten sites share a transport that forwards `source`, `medium` and `campaign` without separating interaction context from acquisition.
INFERENCE: forwarded attribution fields contribute to misleading source reports.
Change: namespace interaction properties and preserve validated landing UTMs in initial GA4 configuration. Retain consent gating, URL redaction, stream selection and manual pageviews.
Definition of done: regression tests, project checks, production build, commit, publish and verify production bundle contains the correction.
Excluded: ad campaign changes, spending, outbound messages and backend changes.
